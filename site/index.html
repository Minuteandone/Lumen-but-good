EPISODE ONE

The station has been dark for a very long time.

Lumen boots up alone beneath years of dust. He has no map, no instructions, and barely enough light to see the room around him. Then one accidental bump wakes a dead lamp.

One light becomes two. Two become a room. And somewhere in all that newly discovered brightness, a tiny moth decides Lumen looks like an excellent place to land.

After a brief period in which both of them panic because the other one is panicking, they figure each other out and restore the first chamber together. For a moment, it feels like the job is finished.

It is very, very much not finished.
